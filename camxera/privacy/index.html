CamXera Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

CamXera is a camera app made by GIN Labs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it on Android or iPhone.

Your photos and videos stay on your phone

Everything you shoot with CamXera is saved only to your phone's own photo library (the "CamXera" album). CamXera has no account, no cloud storage and no server of its own. We never receive, see or keep your photos, videos or sound recordings.

Camera, microphone and photo access

  • Camera: to take photos and videos, and to scan QR codes.
  • Microphone: to record sound with your videos. It is only used while you record.
  • Photos and videos: to save what you shoot, and to show, edit, share and delete the pictures in CamXera's own gallery.

You can turn any of these off at any time in your phone's settings.

Features that analyse the picture

Face smoothing, the eyes-open check, AI auto adjust, the hand-sign shutter, QR code scanning and object removal all run entirely on your phone. The camera picture is never sent anywhere for these features.

Technical information collected by Google ML Kit (Android only)

The Android app uses Google's ML Kit for face detection and QR code scanning. ML Kit may send Google basic technical information, such as the phone model, Android version, which ML features were used and how well they performed. This is used for diagnostics and to improve ML Kit. It does not include your photos, videos or anything that identifies you, and it is sent encrypted. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

The iPhone app uses only Apple's built-in frameworks, which run on the phone.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your phone's own share menu sends the photo to the app you choose. What happens next is governed by that app's privacy policy.

Advertising, tracking and children

CamXera has no ads, no analytics of its own and no tracking. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
