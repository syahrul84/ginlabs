Privacy policy

The short version: mySolat keeps your settings and prayer times on your phone. The developer receives no data about you, and the app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings, your prayer zone, saved locations and the downloaded prayer times.
  • The camera picture used by the rakaat counter. It is analysed on the phone to notice each sujud, and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The camera only runs while the counter screen is open.
  • Whether a phone call is answered during a rakaat count, so the count can end. The app sees only that a call is active, never who is calling.

What leaves your phone, and why

api.waktusolat.appYour prayer zone code. If you choose "Use current location", your coordinates once, to find your JAKIM zone.Updating prayer times; finding your zone

Like any internet request, these services also see your IP address. Each handles data under its own privacy policy. mySolat does not send your location anywhere else, does not keep it, and does not share it with anyone.

Permissions

  • Notifications and alarms: for the reminders and the optional alarm at prayer time.
  • Location: only when you use your current location or open Kiblat. You can always choose a zone by hand instead.
  • Camera: only for the rakaat counter. There is no microphone access at all.

Children

mySolat collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Everything mySolat stores is on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes it.
